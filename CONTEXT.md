# Job Copilot

A job-search assistant that finds roles, scores them against a candidate's real experience, and prepares reviewable application material. A person always makes the final submission.

## Language

**Job Posting**:
A role found at a source.
_Avoid_: Job, listing, lead

**Source**:
The site or service a Job Posting came from.
_Avoid_: Provider, feed

**Watchlist**:
The employers a person chooses to follow, whose job boards are searched for Job Postings.
_Avoid_: Target list, company list

**Match**:
A Job Posting scored against a Profile, including its Interview Likelihood.
_Avoid_: Recommendation, result

**Interview Likelihood**:
The estimated chance that applying to a Job Posting leads to an interview, with its reasoning.
_Avoid_: Match score, fit score

**Application Pack**:
The tailored resume, cover letter and form answers prepared for one Job Posting. A Pack that is never submitted does not count as an Application.
_Avoid_: Draft application, documents

**Proposal**:
The Application Pack format for a freelance Job Posting: a short pitch plus a rate or bid. Planned for after v1.
_Avoid_: Bid, cover letter

**Application**:
The record that a person submitted to a Job Posting. It exists only once they mark the job as applied, and it carries the status and time.
_Avoid_: Submission

**Evidence**:
A specific fact in a Profile or GitHub repository that a claim traces back to.
_Avoid_: Source, proof
